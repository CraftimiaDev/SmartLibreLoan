Itemised Holding Costs Explained

1. Body Corporate / Strata Fees

This fee is a mandatory contribution paid by all owners in a managed complex to maintain shared infrastructure. It is legally split into two separate bank accounts:

  • The Administrative Fund: Covers day-to-day operating costs including common area electricity (lifts, hallways, car parks), lawn mowing, pool cleaning, building manager or caretaker salaries, and basic cleaning supplies.
  • The Sinking / Capital Works Fund: A long-term savings pool tucked away to pay for predictable, expensive structural upgrades down the track, such as repainting building facades, replacing lifts, repairing roofing, or repaving driveways.

2. Council Rates

Council rates are a property tax levied by your local government municipality to fund community infrastructure and local civic services.

3. Water Fixed Rates

Water bills include fixed access charges and volumetric usage charges. This category specifically refers to the fixed component required to keep your property connected to the main water grid.

4. Building / Landlord Insurance

Safeguards your capital investment against catastrophic structural or financial loss.

5. Maintenance Fund Reserve

Routine repairs and breakdown of items over time including plumbing leaks, hot water system blowouts, fence repairs, or replacing air conditioning units.

6. Power / Gas / Internet

Standard retail utility bills covering day-to-day energy consumption and digital connectivity.
